# Narrative sources remain a separate sensitive pack

Future content-supported narrative generation will consume an optional,
digested Narrative Source Pack linked to Assertion and Evidence IDs instead of
placing descriptions, comments, or diffs in the canonical Bundle. Source
priority is description, in-window non-system interaction, commit metadata,
then bounded diff. The private workflow's 20-file, 4000-line, and 1-MiB diff
limits are research inputs rather than a public contract until provider-neutral
privacy, calibration, and verification rules exist; the Agent Skill may not
fetch or construct the pack itself.
